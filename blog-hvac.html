Sizing an electrical panel for future load growth

What to plan for before your panel becomes a bottleneck.

SES / FIELD NOTES04 MIN READ

Efficiency is not a single component. It is the result of a system that has been correctly sized, installed, balanced and understood by the people who run it.

Design around use

Occupancy, heat loads, fresh-air requirements and maintenance access should shape the design from the beginning. A solution that ignores the operating pattern will cost more to run, regardless of its equipment rating.

“The best HVAC system is the one people barely have to think about.”

Commission the details

Airflow, set points, controls and documentation turn a good installation into a dependable one. Testing and handover are where the promise of efficiency becomes something the client can verify.
