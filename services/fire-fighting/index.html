At Supreme Engineering Services (SES), we engineer and execute industrial and commercial fire protection infrastructure designed for absolute readiness. A fire safety system is one asset that cannot afford a failure rate — our in-house engineering team delivers full end-to-end design, pipe routing, fabrication, pump room commissioning, and municipal Fire NOC compliance.

Our Fire Protection Engineering Scope

◌ Fire Hydrant & Wet Riser Systems

Heavy-duty internal and external hydrant networks engineered for immediate high-pressure water delivery during an emergency:

  • External ring mains with landing valves and fire brigade inlets
  • Internal wet risers, hose reel drums, and branch pipes
  • Underground and above-ground MS/GI piping with anti-corrosive coating
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing up to 14 bar

◉ Automatic Fire Sprinkler Networks

Precision heat-activated sprinkler networks designed to suppress and contain fires at the point of origin:

  • Pendant, upright, and sidewall sprinkler heads (UL/FM approved)
  • Alarm check valve (ACV) assemblies and flow switches
  • Zoned distribution networks for commercial towers, warehouses, and factories
  • Hydraulic calculations adhering to NBC 2016 and NFPA standards

ϟ Fire Pump Rooms & Automation

The beating heart of every fire fighting installation, engineered for automated pressure maintenance and instant start:

  • Main electrical fire pump with automatic starter panels
  • Diesel engine backup pump for power failure continuity
  • Jockey pump for automated header pressure maintenance
  • Pressure relief valves, suction/discharge manifolds, and bypass headers

╋ Fire Alarm, Detection & Suppression

Integrated electronic detection and clean-agent specialized suppression systems:

  • Addressable and conventional optical smoke & heat detectors
  • Manual call points (MCP) and multi-tone electronic sounders
  • Gas suppression systems (FM-200 / Novec 1230 / CO2) for server rooms
  • Fire barriers, fire damper integration, and emergency exit signage

Code Compliance & Fire NOC Documentation

We work closely with architects, consultants, and municipal authorities to ensure your facility meets all fire regulations without redesign delays:

  • NBC 2016 (Part 4): Full compliance with Fire and Life Safety classification standards.
  • Hydraulic Calculations: Proof of water flow rate, residual pressure, and tank capacity calculations.
  • Testing & Handover: As-built drawings, hydrostatic test certificates, pump discharge curves, and operator training.
