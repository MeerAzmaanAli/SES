Proper HVAC engineering is not merely selecting an equipment tonnage rating — it is calculating thermal loads, acoustic insulation, airflow dynamics, and seasonal energy efficiency. Supreme Engineering Services (SES) delivers turnkey HVAC infrastructure for hospitals, pharmaceutical facilities, education campuses, and commercial complexes across central India.

Comprehensive HVAC Capabilities

◉ VRV & VRF Multi-Split Systems

Advanced variable refrigerant volume systems providing precise zoned thermal comfort with minimal electrical footprint:

  • Simultaneous heating and cooling heat-recovery systems
  • Modular outdoor units suitable for high-rise commercial structures
  • Smart central touch controllers and BMS integration
  • Copper refrigerant piping engineered with zero-leak brazing standards

◇ Pharmaceutical Cleanrooms & PUFF Panels

Strict environmental control complying with WHO-GMP and ISO cleanroom classification standards:

  • Positive and negative pressure room cascading
  • HEPA and ultra-fine filtration units with terminal diffusers
  • Modular PUFF wall and ceiling insulated cleanroom panels
  • Humidity and temperature precision control for formulation labs

╋ In-House Ductwork Fabrication & Airflow

Custom ducting engineered to exact site drawings, cutting delivery times and field errors:

  • Galvanized Iron (GI) and pre-insulated duct (PID) fabrication
  • Motorized volume control dampers (VCD) and fire dampers
  • Acoustic and thermal duct insulation (nitrile rubber and glasswool)
  • Airflow balancing using calibrated digital anemometers

⌁ Central Chilled Water Plants

High-capacity cooling infrastructure for institutional and industrial sites:

  • Air-cooled and water-cooled scroll/screw chillers
  • Air Handling Units (AHU) and Fan Coil Units (FCU)
  • Primary and secondary chilled water pumping systems
  • Condenser water cooling towers and treated water piping
