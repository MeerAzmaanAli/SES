Cold Rooms & Industrial Refrigeration in Indore & MP

Engineered cold-chain infrastructure, insulated PUFF panels, condensing units, and controlled-atmosphere storage facilities.

SES / COLD ROOMS HIGH-DENSITY PUFF PANELS TEMPERATURE CONTROLLED

Cold storage is more than dropping temperatures inside a room — it is preserving product integrity through calculated relative humidity, thermal insulation thickness, and door seal efficiency. Supreme Engineering Services (SES) designs and executes commercial cold rooms, ripening chambers, and cold warehouses for agro-produce, dates, pharmaceuticals, and dairy.

Cold Room Engineering Capabilities

◇ Modular PUFF Panels & Doors

High-density Polyurethane Foam (PUFF) insulated modular tongue-and-groove cam-lock panels:

  • 60mm, 80mm, 100mm, 120mm, and 150mm panel options
  • Pre-painted galvanized steel (PPGI) and stainless steel finishes
  • Heavy-duty sliding and swing insulated doors with thermal break gaskets
  • Heated door frames to eliminate condensation and ice formation

◉ Refrigeration Machinery & Condensing Units

Robust industrial refrigeration packages engineered for tropical climates:

  • High-efficiency semi-hermetic and scroll condensing units
  • Ceiling-suspended heavy-duty forced-draft evaporators
  • Microprocessor-based automatic defrost cycles (air/electric)
  • Eco-friendly low-GWP refrigerants (R404A / R407C / R134a)

⌁ Specialized Perishable Storage

Tailored temperature and humidity profiles for specialized commodities:

  • Dates and dried fruit storage (+0°C to +4°C) with controlled moisture
  • Pharma stability chambers (+2°C to +8°C) with dual-redundant machinery
  • Deep-freeze chambers (-18°C to -25°C) for frozen foods
  • Automated temperature data logging and remote alert systems
