What NBC 2016 actually requires for warehouse sprinklers

A practical breakdown of fire code compliance for storage facilities.

SES / FIELD NOTES05 MIN READ

A fire protection system has one job: to be ready before anyone knows they need it. That makes the quality of the engineering, installation and commissioning more important than how impressive the equipment schedule looks.

Start with the building’s reality

Occupancy, travel paths, storage height, access for emergency teams and the way people actually use a space should shape the system. A good design responds to the building instead of forcing the building to work around a generic layout.

“Compliance is the starting line. Readiness is the standard.”

Connect every layer

Detection, alarms, hydrants, sprinklers, signage and emergency procedures work best when they are planned as one coordinated system. The interfaces matter: power, water supply, access and clear responsibility during an event.

Commission for confidence

Testing is where intent becomes evidence. SES approaches handover as a practical operating exercise, with documentation and demonstrations that help the people on site know what to do next.
