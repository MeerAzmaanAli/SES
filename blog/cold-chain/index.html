FIELD NOTE · COLD STORAGE

Domestic vs. industrial RO — what actually changes

Capacity, maintenance, and compliance differences worth knowing.

SES / FIELD NOTES04 MIN READ

There is no universal cold room. The right design begins with what is being stored, how often the room is accessed and what the operating day really looks like.

Temperature is only one requirement

Humidity, product load, door cycles, insulation, drainage and monitoring all influence performance. The panel is important, but it is only one part of a complete storage environment.

“Protect the product first. The system follows.”

Plan for the people using it

Safe access, visibility, service clearances and simple controls make a cold room more dependable in everyday use. Good engineering respects both the product and the operator.
